This policy explains how Biddex collects, uses, and protects personal data in connection with the Biddex B2B procurement marketplace, in line with Bahrain's Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL, Law No. 30 of 2018).

1. Who this applies to

This policy covers personal data of individuals who register a company account on Biddex, act as a contact for a registered company, or otherwise interact with the platform (buyers, suppliers, and their staff).

2. Data we collect

CategoryExamples
Account dataEmail, password (stored as a hash), role (buyer/supplier)
Company dataCompany name, type, registration number, country, address, phone
Verification documentsCommercial registration and related files you upload
Transaction dataRFQs, quotes, LPOs, orders, invoices, wallet transactions
CommunicationsMessages and notifications sent through the platform
Usage dataLogin times, basic technical data needed to operate the service

3. Why we process your data

We process personal data to:

  • Create and manage your account and company profile;
  • Operate the RFQ, quote, LPO, order, and payment workflow;
  • Verify companies and prevent fraud;
  • Send account-related and transactional notifications;
  • Meet legal and regulatory obligations in Bahrain;
  • Maintain the security of the platform.

4. Legal basis

We process your personal data on the basis of your consent (given at registration), the necessity of processing to perform our contract with you, and our legitimate interest in operating and securing the platform, consistent with the PDPL.

5. Sharing your data

Company profile information you choose to make visible (such as your company name and catalog) is shared with other companies on the platform as part of the RFQ and quoting process. We do not sell personal data. We may share data with service providers who help us run the platform (such as hosting and database providers) under appropriate safeguards, or where required by law.

6. Data storage and security

Data is stored with reputable infrastructure providers and protected with measures including password hashing, access controls, and rate limiting on authentication endpoints. No system is completely secure, and we work to continuously improve these protections.

7. Data retention and account deactivation

When you deactivate your account, your company is marked inactive and you can no longer sign in or transact on the platform. We retain the underlying records for a period afterward where needed to meet legal, accounting, or dispute-resolution obligations, after which data is deleted or anonymized in line with our retention practice.

8. Your rights under the PDPL

Subject to the conditions set out in the PDPL, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you;
  • Request correction of inaccurate data;
  • Withdraw consent and request deactivation of your account;
  • Request erasure of your data, subject to our legal retention obligations;
  • Object to certain processing.

To exercise these rights, contact us using the details below.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be indicated by an updated "Last updated" date above.
